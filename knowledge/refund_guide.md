# Vireo Refund AI Knowledge Base

## Purpose

Vireo Refund AI is a refund intelligence and support analytics dashboard.

It analyzes:
- refund volume
- refund amount
- refund reasons
- support teams
- support channels
- agents
- customer tickets
- orders
- refund trends

## Refund Reasons

Common refund reason codes include:

- GW-OTHER
- RETURN-QC-OK
- DUP-PAYMENT
- CANCEL
- DOA-REPL
- WTY-BUYBACK
- PRICE-ADJ
- LOST-TRANSIT

## Support Teams

The dashboard can analyze teams including:

- Billing
- Chat Frontline
- Email Frontline
- Returns Desk
- Logistics
- Escalations & Warranty
- Voice Frontline

## Support Channels

Supported channels include:

- chat
- email
- social
- voice

## Analysis

The dashboard provides:

- overall ticket volume
- refund ticket volume
- refund amount
- average refund
- refund rate
- average CSAT
- refunds by reason
- refunds by channel
- monthly refund trend
- refunds by team
- agent refund analysis
- ticket-level refund exploration
- data reconciliation
- business snapshot
